1. How often is the data updated?

The frequency and timing of the publication of new data vary depending on the individual indicators. As soon as new data for an indicator becomes available, or previously published data is revised, it is updated in this dashboard.

The dashboard is usually updated once a month. You can see when the last update took place under the Details tab in the Data state section.

2. How can I suggest new or alternative data sources?

If you would like to provide feedback on the data sources used or suggest new ones, please contact us at {{site.email_contacts.questions}}

3. What do the symbols and letters next to the data in the charts and tables mean?

If the numerical values of individual data points cannot be published, or cannot be published with their original values, a so-called value-replacement character (a character in place of numbers) is used instead of the numerical value. These characters appear exclusively in the table view.

In certain cases, numerical values require an explanation, for example to clarify the significance or the origin of the numerical value. In such cases, a so-called value-explanatory symbol (a symbol next to the numbers) is used in addition to the numerical value.

In the graph view, value-explanatory symbols appear when you hover the mouse over the relevant data point. In the table view, they are appended to the numerical values.

The meaning of the symbols used is explained in the legend below the graph and the table.

4. What is disaggregation?

Disaggregation involves breaking down an existing indicator into further subcategories for a more detailed analysis. Examples include breaking down data by age group or sex.

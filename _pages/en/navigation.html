--- title: Structure of the dashboard permalink: /en/help/navigation/ language: en layout: page --- The dashboard is divided into four categories, 22 sections and 31 provisional ‘Beyond GDP’ indicators from the United Nations High-Level Expert Group on Beyond GDP.

Presentation of the individual indicators

For each area, the relevant data from the available time series for a given indicator are presented both in a graph and in a table. This presentation is supplemented by the display f data. Where possible, explanatory texts are provided with links to further, thematically relevant information. The green buttons below the graph can be used to download the displayed data in CSV format and the graph as a PNG file.

Details and Data sources

The tabs Details and Data sources are located at the bottom of the respective indicator page.
  • The Details tab contains the indicator name as well as information on the available time series, the scope and the data status. In addition, for some indicators, explanations on how to interpret them correctly are provided here.
  • Under Data sources, you will find links to the organisations that provide the data for the time series. Where possible, links to the specific data sources are also provided.

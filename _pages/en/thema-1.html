--- layout: goal-group title: Thema 1 goal_number: '1' language: en permalink: /en/thema-1/ --- Peace, human rights and respect for the planet form the foundation of societal progress. They are essential prerequisites for a life of dignity, security and self-determination, as well as for sustainable development

In the Beyond GDP framework proposed by the Independent High-Level Expert Group, these foundational principles provide the overarching frame of reference. They serve as the normative basis for assessing societal progress and highlight that progress should not be measured solely in terms of economic performance. Rather, it is also about creating and maintaining societal conditions that enable human well-being, promote social justice and safeguard the ecological foundations of life over the long term. These principles emphasise that progress can only be sustainable if it meets the needs of present generations without compromising the life chances and development opportunities of future generations.

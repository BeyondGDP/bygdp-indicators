--- layout: goal-group title: Thema 1 goal_number: '1' language: en permalink: /en/thema-1/ --- Peace, respect for human rights and the protection of the natural environment form the foundation of social progress. They are fundamental prerequisites for a life of dignity, security and self-determination, as well as for sustainable development.

In the ‘Beyond GDP’ framework proposed by the independent UN expert group, these fundamental principles form the overarching framework. They serve as a normative benchmark for assessing societal progress and make it clear that this should not be measured solely by economic performance. Rather, it is also a matter of creating and maintaining social conditions that enable human well-being, promote social justice and safeguard the ecological foundations of life in the long term. The principles emphasise that progress can only be sustainable if it takes into account the needs of present generations without compromising the opportunities for life and development of future generations.

--- layout: goal-group title: Thema 2 goal_number: '2' language: en permalink: /en/thema-2/ --- Within the framework of the ‘Beyond GDP’ concept proposed by the independent UN expert group, ‘current well-being’ describes people’s quality of life and the social conditions under which they live today. It encompasses far more than material living conditions and economic performance. It should also take into account opportunities for participation in social life, access to education and healthcare, good working conditions, personal safety and social cohesion. The quality of public institutions, the quality of the environment and subjective well-being also have a significant influence on how people perceive and shape their lives.

The approach is based on a multidimensional view of well-being. The focus is on the extent to which economic, social, institutional and environmental conditions enable a good life for all people. The aim is not only to consider the average level of prosperity, but also to examine how different population groups benefit from social progress and what differences exist in their living conditions. The proposed framework of indicators aims to capture the actual quality of life of people in its various facets and to highlight developments that go beyond gross domestic product. The dimension of current well-being is thus intended to illustrate what social development actually means for people today.

--- layout: goal-group title: Thema 2 goal_number: '2' language: en permalink: /en/thema-2/ --- In the context of the Beyond GDP framework proposed by the UN Secretary-General's Independent High-Level Expert Group on Beyond GDP, current well-being describes people's quality of life and the societal conditions in which they live today. It encompasses far more than material living conditions and economic performance. It also takes into account opportunities for participation in society, access to education and healthcare, decent work, personal security and social cohesion. The quality of institutions, environmental quality and subjective well-being are also key factors influencing how people experience and shape their lives.

The approach is based on a multidimensional understanding of well-being. Its central concern is the extent to which economic, social, institutional and environmental conditions enable all people to lead good lives. This requires looking not only at average levels of well-being, but also at how different population groups share in societal progress and at the disparities in their living conditions. The proposed dashboard of indicators aims to capture people's actual quality of life across its various dimensions and to highlight developments that go beyond Gross Domestic Product (GDP). The current well-being component is therefore intended to illustrate what societal progress actually means for people today.

--- layout: goal-group title: Thema 3 goal_number: '3' language: en permalink: /en/thema-3/ --- A just society is characterised by the fact that all people, regardless of their social background, gender, age or individual circumstances, have equal opportunities to lead self-determined lives and participate in society. Equality and participation are therefore essential prerequisites for social cohesion and sustainable progress.

By focusing on equality and participation, the UN expert group addresses a central aspect of its ‘Beyond GDP’ approach: it is not enough to consider the average well-being of a society. Equally crucial is the question of how prosperity, income, wealth, resources and opportunities for development are distributed, and who actually benefits from social progress. The proposed approach therefore focuses in particular on the prevention of poverty and social exclusion, equality of opportunity, equal access to education, work and social services, as well as opportunities to participate in social life. A comprehensive assessment should also take into account existing inequalities between different population groups and their impact on individual life prospects. Social progress should therefore not be assessed solely on the basis of rising prosperity, but also according to the extent to which as many people as possible can share in its achievements and no one is left behind.

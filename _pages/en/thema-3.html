--- layout: goal-group title: Thema 3 goal_number: '3' language: en permalink: /en/thema-3/ --- A just society is one in which all people, regardless of their socio-economic background, gender, age or individual circumstances, have equal opportunities to lead self-determined lives and participate fully in society. Equity and inclusion are therefore essential prerequisites for social cohesion and sustainable progress.

Through the equity and inclusion component, the Independent High-Level Expert Group addresses a central aspect of its Beyond GDP approach: assessing the average level of well-being in a society is not sufficient. Equally important is the question of how prosperity, income, wealth, resources and opportunities for development are distributed, and who actually benefits from societal progress. The proposed framework therefore focuses in particular on addressing poverty and social exclusion, promoting equality of opportunity and ensuring equitable access to education, employment and opportunities for participation in society. A comprehensive assessment should also take account of existing inequalities between different population groups and their impact on individual life chances. Societal progress should therefore not be assessed solely in terms of rising levels of prosperity, but also according to the extent to which all people can share in its benefits and no one is left behind.

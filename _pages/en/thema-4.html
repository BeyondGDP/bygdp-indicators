--- layout: goal-group title: Thema 4 goal_number: '4' language: en permalink: /en/thema-4/ --- Within the framework established by the independent UN expert group, sustainability and resilience describe the extent to which a society is able to secure the foundations of its livelihood and development in the long term, and to remain capable of taking action even under changing circumstances and in the face of crises and challenges.

At the heart of the framework developed by the expert group lies the question of whether today’s well-being can be secured in the long term without compromising the opportunities for life and development of future generations. To this end, in addition to economic and financial resources, particular account should be taken of a society’s natural, human, social and institutional capital. The protection of the climate, ecosystems and biodiversity, the responsible management of natural resources, and investment in education, health, social cohesion and effective institutions are key factors in assessing sustainable development. Resilience also encompasses the ability to identify risks at an early stage, respond appropriately to crises and successfully manage the necessary adaptation processes. Social progress should therefore not be assessed solely on the basis of current living conditions, but also on whether the foundations for prosperity, stability and well-being can be maintained and strengthened in the long term.
